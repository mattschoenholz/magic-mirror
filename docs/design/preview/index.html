Web pages cannot close Chromium in kiosk mode. Use one of:

From your Mac (copy command):

ssh pi@mirror-pi4.local 'pkill -f chromium || pkill -f chromium-browser || true'

USB keyboard on the Pi: Alt+F4 (may vary by desktop).

Windowed preview (browser chrome back):

PREVIEW_WINDOWED=1 ./scripts/sync-design-preview-to-pi.sh --open

Wood frame overlaps ~20–30px of the LCD — keep tappable controls inside the inset dock (see preview-dock.css / tokens.md).
